# Project Technical Assistant (Extension)

This extension turns MomAI into an intelligent assistant to help you manage and understand your project files. It is the perfect tool for anyone who needs to analyze code, organize folders, or make quick edits with the help of artificial intelligence.

### What it does for you:
*   **File Reading**: It reads and explains what is written in your files clearly.
*   **Smart Editing**: Helps you change specific parts of a text or code without messing up the rest of the file.
*   **Advanced Search**: Locates words or concepts in all your files at once.
*   **Organization Support**: Helps you understand your folder structure and suggests improvements.

### Safety First:
No changes are made to your computer without your **explicit authorization**. MomAI will always show you what it intends to change and wait for you to click "Confirm" before saving anything.

### Examples of how to use:
- "Explain how this configuration file works."
- "Where do I change the main color of my website in this project?"
- "Create a new text file with a task list."
- "Find where I mentioned the word 'database' in my scripts."

### Note:
Ideal for technology professionals or anyone who needs extra help dealing with complex files.
