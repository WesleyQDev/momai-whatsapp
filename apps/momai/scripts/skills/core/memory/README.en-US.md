# Long-Term Memory (System Component)

This component allows MomAI to remember important information about you, your preferences, and past conversations. It is what makes your assistant increasingly personalized and intelligent over time.

### What it does for you:
*   **Fact Memory**: Stores information such as family names, important dates, or preferences (e.g., "Remember that I like coffee without sugar").
*   **Continuous Context**: Allows the assistant to know what you are talking about based on previous interactions.
*   **Semantic Search**: Finds stored information even if you don't use the exact words.

### Usage examples:
- "Remember that my Monday meeting is at 10 am."
- "What was the name of the restaurant I mentioned last week?"
- "Forget that I live in São Paulo."

### Privacy:
You have full control over what MomAI remembers. You can ask to see what it knows or request that it delete specific information at any time.
