# Internet Search (System Component)

This functionality allows MomAI to consult the internet to answer your questions with always-updated information. It is ideal for knowing the latest news, checking facts, or researching any new topic.

### What it does for you:

- **Search Current Information**: Finds answers to things that just happened.
- **Diverse Sources**: Analyzes various sites to give you a complete answer.
- **Citations**: It shows you where the information came from so you can trust what you are reading.

### Examples of what you can ask:

- "What is today's technology news?"
- "Who won the football game last night?"
- "What is the current price of the dollar?"
- "Tell me the latest news about artificial intelligence."

### Note:

This is a native component of MomAI and comes pre-configured to ensure your assistant always has the right answer.
