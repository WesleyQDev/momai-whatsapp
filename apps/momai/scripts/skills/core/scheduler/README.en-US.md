# Scheduler and Reminders (System Component)

This component allows MomAI to manage your appointments and reminders. It ensures you never miss an important task or a scheduled appointment.

### What it does for you:

- **Reminder Creation**: Sets alerts for specific times (e.g., "Remind me to drink water every 2 hours").
- **Task Management**: Organizes your to-do list with defined deadlines.
- **Smart Notifications**: Alerts you at the exact moment, even if the app is in the background.

### Usage examples:

- "Remind me to call the doctor tomorrow at 3 pm."
- "Schedule a reminder to take the cake out of the oven in 20 minutes."
- "What are my reminders for today?"

### Note:

This is an essential component for keeping your routine organized with the help of MomAI.
