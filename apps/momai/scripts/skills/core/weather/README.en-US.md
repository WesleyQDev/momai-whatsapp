# Weather and Forecast (System Component)

This feature allows MomAI to inform you about the weather anywhere in the world. It is perfect for knowing if you need to bring a coat or if the day will be good for an outdoor walk.

### What it shows for you:

- **Temperature**: The exact temperature and how it actually feels (thermal sensation).
- **Sky Conditions**: If the day is sunny, cloudy, raining, or snowing.
- **Day Forecast**: What to expect for the next few hours or for the next days of the week.

### Examples of what you can ask:

- "How is the weather now in New York?"
- "Will it rain in London tomorrow?"
- "What is the forecast for the weekend in Miami?"
- "Do I need to take an umbrella today?"

### Note:

This is a native component of MomAI, designed to work quickly and display easy-to-read visual cards directly in your chat.
